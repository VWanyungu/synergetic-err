<Frame><img src="/images/profile.png" alt="Profile page" /></Frame>

Open your avatar (top right) to reach your profile.

- Change your **photo** (camera icon on the avatar) and **banner**.
- **My access** lists your role, scope, who granted it and when. Only an Admin or Risk Officer can change it.

The moon icon in the top bar switches between light and dark mode.
