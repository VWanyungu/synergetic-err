The top bar is the same for everyone, but the items shown depend on your role.

| Item | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Everyone except Division Leads |
| **Registers** | Everyone except Division Leads, who see **My division** instead |
| **Heat map** | Everyone except Division Leads |
| **Reports** | Everyone except Division Leads |
| **New risk** | Everyone except Division Leads |
| **Admin** | Admins and Risk Officers |
| Bell (notifications), avatar (profile, log out), moon icon (light/dark) | Everyone |

## Global search

Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl K</kbd> anywhere to search risks by title, ID or owner. You only find risks inside your own scope.

## Registers

See [Risk registers](/common/registers) for browsing, filtering and sorting.

## Scope

- **Risk Champion and Department Lead** see their own department.
- **Division Lead** sees risks affecting their division, read only.
- **Risk Officer and Admin** see every department and division.
