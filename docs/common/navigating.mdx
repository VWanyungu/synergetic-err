## The top bar

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Review compliance, mitigation coverage and the risk mix at a glance. |
| **Registers** | Lists every risk by department or division. |
| **Heat map** | Shows where risks cluster by impact and likelihood. |
| **Reports** | Opens the monthly snapshots of the register. |
| **Admin** | Manages users and access. |
| **New risk** | Logs a new risk. |
| Search box | Finds a risk by title, ID or owner. |
| Moon icon | Switches between light and dark mode. |
| Bell | Opens your [notifications](/common/notifications). |
| Avatar | Opens your [profile](/common/profile) and **Log out**. |

## Global search

Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl K</kbd> anywhere to search risks by title, ID or owner. You only find risks inside your own scope.

See [Risk registers](/common/registers) for browsing, filtering and sorting.

## Scope

The menu only shows what your account can use.

- **Risk Champion and Department Lead** see their own department.
- **Division Lead** sees risks affecting their division, read only, through **My division**.
- **Risk Officer and Admin** see every department and division.
