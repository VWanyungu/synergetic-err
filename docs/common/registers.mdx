Hover on **Registers** in the top bar. The **Departments** and **Divisions** tabs list every register; choose one to open it.

<Frame><img src="/images/Screenshot from 2026-11-27 11-14-56.png" alt="Department risk register for Commercial" /></Frame>

## Find a risk

- **Search** by title, ID or owner. Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl K</kbd> anywhere for global search.
- Expand a row with the arrow for a quick summary, or select it to open the [risk detail](/common/risk-detail).
    <Frame><img src="/images/expanded risk.png" alt="Department risk register for Commercial" /></Frame>
- Use the breadcrumb switcher (next to the register name) to jump between departments without going back.
    <Frame><img src="/images/department switcher.png" alt="Department risk register for Commercial" /></Frame>

## Filters

Active filters show as chips above the list; **Clear filters** resets them.

Filters work together: each one narrows the results left by the others. For example, selecting a category and a rating shows only risks that match both.

<Frame><img src="/images/register-filters.png" alt="Register filters" /></Frame>

| Filter | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Shows only risks in one category. |
| **Mitigation** | Narrows by how the risk is being handled. **Accept**, **Mitigate**, **Transfer** and **Avoid** show risks with that [risk response](/reference/scoring). **No mitigation plan** shows risks that have no mitigation plan at all. |
| **Period** | Sets the month shown throughout the app. When you change it anywhere, all tables use the selected period; in registers, it shows each risk's rating for that month and sets the month used by **Last review**. |
| **Order** | Sorts the list: latest, highest or lowest rating, last reviewed, title, department, owner or status. |

The column headers are additional filters:

| Column | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Department** | Limits the list to one department (not shown if you only see your own). |
| **Rating** | Filters to **High**, **Medium** or **Low** risks (see [scoring](/reference/scoring)). The arrows beside it sort by rating. |
| **Owner** | Shows one person's risks. |
| **Status** | Shows risks that are still open (**Active**), closed (**Inactive**) or **Archived**. |
| **Last review** | **Reviewed** shows risks with a review logged in the selected period. **Not reviewed** shows the ones still waiting. |
