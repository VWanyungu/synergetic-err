The bell in the top bar shows a dot when you have unread items. Open it to see **All** notifications or only **Unread**, and use **Mark all as read** to clear the list.

## Archive requests

When a Risk Champion or Department Lead asks to archive a risk, every Risk Officer and Admin gets a notification like this one.

<Frame><img src="/images/notifications-archive-request.png" alt="An archive request notification with Accept, Reject and View risk" /></Frame>

Each request shows who asked and which risk it is about. From here you can:

- **Accept** to archive the risk.
- **Reject** to keep it in the register.
- **View risk** to open it first. The same **Accept** and **Reject** buttons also appear in a banner at the top of the risk.

<Frame><img src="/images/archive-request-banner.png" alt="Archive request banner on the risk page" /></Frame>

## Outcomes

Once a request is decided, the person who asked gets a notification saying whether the risk was archived or kept in the register, and the request shows **Accepted** or **Rejected** with the name of who decided it.
