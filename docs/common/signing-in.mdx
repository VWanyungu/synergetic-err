---
title: "Signing in"
description: "Sign in, sign out and fix the most common sign-in problems."
---

Accounts are created for you by an Admin or Risk Officer; there is no public sign-up. Use the address for the [environment](/#two-environments) you need.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the portal">
    Go to the Risk Register address you were given.
    <Frame><img src="/images/login.png" alt="Login page" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Type your work email and password, then select **Log in**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Signing out

Open your avatar (top right) and choose **Log out**.

<Frame><img src="/images/logout.png" alt="Account menu with My profile and Log out" /></Frame>

## Common sign-in problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Incorrect email or password">
    Check the email for typos and that Caps Lock is off, then [reset your password](/common/resetting-password) if you still cannot get in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The password is right but I am still refused">
    Your account may be deactivated. Ask an Admin or Risk Officer to check it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I was sent back to the login page">
    Your session has ended, or an Admin changed your access, which signs you out. Sign in again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I signed in on staging with my production password, or the reverse">
    Staging and production are separate, so an account or password change in one does not carry to the other. Reset it in the environment you are using.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I do not have an account">
    Ask an Admin or Risk Officer to create one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
