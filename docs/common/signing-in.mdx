---
title: "Signing in"
description: "Sign in, sign out and what to do when you cannot get in."
---

Everyone signs in the same way, whatever their role. Accounts are created by an Admin or Risk Officer; there is no public sign-up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the portal">
    Go to the Risk Register address your organisation gave you.
    <Frame><img src="/images/login.png" alt="Login page" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Type your work email and password, then select **Log in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on your home page">
    Most roles open on the dashboard. A [Division Lead](/roles/division-lead/overview) opens straight on their division's register.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To sign out, open your avatar (top right) and choose **Log out**.

## If you cannot sign in

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Incorrect email or password** | Check for typos, then [reset your password](/common/resetting-password). |
| Right password, still refused | Your account may be deactivated. Ask an Admin or Risk Officer. |
| Sent back to the login page with a session-expired notice | Your session ended. Sign in again. |
| No account yet | Ask an Admin or Risk Officer to create one. |

<Note>Changing your role, department or division signs you out so the change applies. Sign in again to continue.</Note>
