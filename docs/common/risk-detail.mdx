## Tabs

- **Overview:** description, activity log, and comments.
- **Mitigation plans:** what is being done, who owns it, when it is due and its status.
- **Analytics:** how the risk's rating has changed over time.

## Side panel

Rating, response, category, affected divisions and owner. Use the gear icon beside each to change it. Every change is recorded in **Activity** with who made it and the old and new values.

<Frame><img src="/images/risk-sidebar.png" alt="Side panel with review, transfer, archive and copy link" /></Frame>

## Review

The **Review** section shows when the risk was last reviewed. Click **Log review** after you have gone through the risk; it records the review in **Activity** and updates **Last reviewed**. See [Mitigation plans and reviews](/roles/department/mitigation-and-review).

## Comments

Write in Markdown or free text and attach files by pasting, dropping or clicking. Posted comments can be edited by the commentor but cannot be deleted.

## Closing a risk

Select **Close risk** beside the comment box once the risk is dealt with. Closing sets the risk response to **Accept** and status to inactive. Select **Open risk** to reopen it (the response stays as it is).

## Transferring a risk

Choose **Transfer risk** in the side panel, pick the new department and confirm. The new department then owns the risk and is the only one that can edit it. The old department can still read it and comment, and the transfer is logged in the risk activity.

<Frame><img src="/images/transfer-risk.png" alt="Transfer risk dialog" /></Frame>

## Archiving a risk

Archiving takes a risk out of the registers and the heat map.

- A Risk Officer or Admin can archive a risk straight away by clicking the **Archive risk** button.
- The Risk Officer approves/denys archive requests by Risk Champions/Department Leads. See [Closing and archiving](/roles/department/closing-and-archiving).

Archived risks are found under the **Archived** status filter in the register, and a Risk Officer or Admin can bring one back with **Unarchive risk**.

## Copy link

**Copy link** puts the risk's address on your clipboard so you can share it. Whoever opens it needs access to that risk.
