---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Sign in and log your first risk in a few minutes."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the portal and enter your work email and password. Accounts are created by an Admin; there is no public sign-up.

    <Frame><img src="/images/login.png" alt="Login page" /></Frame>

    Forgot your password? Choose **Forgot password?** and we will email a reset link, valid for 15 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your register">
    Open **Registers** in the top bar and pick your department (or division). See [Risk registers](/guides/risk-registers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log a risk">
    Select **New risk**, fill in the title, category, department, impact and likelihood, then save. See [Creating a risk](/guides/creating-a-risk).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a mitigation plan and comment">
    Open the risk, add its mitigation plan, and comment after each review. See [Risk detail](/guides/risk-detail).
  </Step>
</Steps>
