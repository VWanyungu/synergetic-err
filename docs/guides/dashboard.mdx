## Filters

- **Department** narrows every widget to one department.
- **Period** shows the dashboard as it stood in a given month.
- The **download** button exports the current view.

## Widgets

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Review compliance">
    The share of risks reviewed this month. A risk counts as reviewed once a comment is added to it that month, so there is no separate "mark reviewed" step. Select the arrow to see the pending list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mitigation coverage">
    How many risks have a mitigation plan (**With plan**) versus none (**No plan**).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Risk breakdown">
    Stacked bars of High (red), Medium (amber) and Low (green) risks, grouped by **Department**, **Category** or **Division**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Risk level distribution">
    Total risks split into High, Medium and Low with counts and percentages. **View details** opens the heat map.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Highest rated risks">
    The top-rated risks with a link to each one. **View all** opens the full register.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
