## Manage users

- **New user** creates an account. Admin-created accounts start with a default password; ask the person to reset it using **Forgot password?**.
- Search by name or email, and filter by **Role** or **Status**.
- Open a user to edit their name, email, role, department or division, or to deactivate them.

## Roles

| Role | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Admin | Full control of users and data. |
| Risk Officer | Organisation-wide oversight. |
| Risk Champion | Maintains one department's risks. |
| Department Lead | Leads a department. |
| Division Lead | Leads a division. |

## Deactivating a user

Deactivated users cannot sign in, but their history stays on every risk they touched.
