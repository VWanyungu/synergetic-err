Each cell is a likelihood (across) and impact (up) combination, showing its rating and how many risks sit there. Cell colour follows the [level](/scoring): green Low, amber Medium, red High.

Filter by **Department**, **Period** and **Rating**. The list beside the grid shows the matching risks, highest first; expand any row for details.
