Open **Registers** in the top bar. The **Departments** and **Divisions** tabs list every register; choose one to open it.

<Frame><img src="/images/department-register.png" alt="Department risk register for Commercial" /></Frame>

## Find a risk

- **Search** by title, ID or owner. Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl K</kbd> anywhere for global search.
- **Filters:** Category, Mitigation, Period and Order. Active filters show as chips; **Clear filters** resets them.
- **Sort** by Department, Rating, Owner, Status or Last review using the column headers.
- Expand a row with the arrow for a quick summary, or select it to open the [risk detail](/guides/risk-detail).

Use the breadcrumb switcher (next to the register name) to jump between departments without going back.

## Division registers

<Frame><img src="/images/division-register.png" alt="Division risk register" /></Frame>

A risk can affect several divisions, so it may appear in more than one division register.
