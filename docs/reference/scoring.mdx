---
title: "Risk scoring"
---

**Rating = Impact × Likelihood**, each scored 1 to 5. The rating is a calculated field based on impact and likelihood inputs.

| Rating | Level |
|---|---|
| 1 – 8 | Low |
| 9 – 14 | Medium |
| 15 – 25 | High |

A risk with no impact or likelihood has no rating and no level until both are set.
