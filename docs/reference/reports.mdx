---
title: "Reports"
description: "Monthly snapshots of the register."
---

<Frame><img src="/images/reports.png" alt="Reports page with monthly tiles" /></Frame>

Pick a **Year**. Each month with a report shows how many risks were on record; months without one are greyed out. Select a month to open that snapshot.

<Frame><img src="/images/report-month.png" alt="A monthly report" /></Frame>

Use the download button to export a report. Reports reflect the register as it was in that month, so later edits do not change them.
