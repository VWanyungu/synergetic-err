## Access

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get an account?">
    Ask an Admin or Risk Officer. There is no public sign-up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password.">
    See [Resetting your password](/common/resetting-password).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot sign in even with the right password.">
    Your account may be deactivated. Contact an Admin or Risk Officer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see the Admin menu?">
    It is shown to Admins and Risk Officers only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Risks

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is a risk's rating calculated?">
    Impact × Likelihood, each 1 to 5. 1–8 is Low, 9–14 is Medium, 15–25 is High. See [Risk scoring](/reference/scoring).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I type the rating or level myself?">
    No. It is always calculated so every screen agrees.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does my risk have no rating?">
    Impact or likelihood is still blank. Set both.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do IDs like COM-06 mean?">
    The department prefix plus a sequence number. IDs are never reused or renumbered.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A risk affects several divisions. Do I create it multiple times?">
    No. Tag it with every affected division; it appears in each division register.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I close a risk?">
    Open it and select **Close risk**. It becomes Inactive but still counts in past periods. Closed risks can be reopened.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit or delete a comment?">
    No. Comments are a permanent record. Add a new comment to correct one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see who changed what?">
    In the **Activity** section of the risk's Overview tab.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reviews and reports

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does a risk count as reviewed?">
    Open the risk and select **Log review** in the side panel. Comments do not count as a review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a month missing from Reports?">
    No snapshot exists for it yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will a past report change if I edit a risk?">
    No. Reports reflect the register as it was in that month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I view the dashboard for a previous month?">
    Yes. Use the **Period** filter.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A page is stuck loading or shows an error.">
    Refresh once. If it persists, sign out and back in, then contact an Admin with the page address and the time it happened.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't find a risk.">
    Use global search (<kbd>Ctrl K</kbd>), clear register filters, and check the **Period** filter is set to the right month.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
