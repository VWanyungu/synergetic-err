A Division Lead has **read-only** access to one division. You see every risk tagged with your division, including risks owned by several departments.

## Your home page

After you [sign in](/common/signing-in) you land on **My division**, your division's register.

<Frame><img src="/images/division-register.png" alt="Division risk register" /></Frame>

## What you can do

- **Browse the register:** search, filter and sort as described in [Risk registers](/common/registers).
- **Open a risk:** read its rating, mitigation plans, comments and activity. See [Risk detail](/common/risk-detail).
- **Use global search** (<kbd>Ctrl K</kbd>) to find a risk in your division.
- **Check [notifications](/common/notifications) and your [profile](/common/profile).**

## What you cannot do

You cannot create, edit, comment on, close or archive risks, and you do not have the dashboard, heat map or reports. Any other address sends you back to your division register.

<Tip>If you need a change to a risk, ask the department's Risk Champion or a Risk Officer. If your register is empty, your account may have no division set; ask an Admin or Risk Officer.</Tip>
