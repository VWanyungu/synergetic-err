---
title: "Archive requests"
description: "Review and decide requests to archive a risk."
---

A Risk Champion or Department Lead cannot archive directly. They send a request and you decide it.

## Decide a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the notification">
    The bell in the top bar shows unread items. Open [Notifications](/common/notifications) to see each request with its status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the risk">
    Open the risk. A banner at the top names who asked and when.
    <Frame><img src="/images/archive-request.png" alt="Archive request banner" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or reject">
    **Accept** archives the risk. **Reject** keeps it in the register. Either way the requester is notified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Archive or restore directly

On any risk, open the gear menu and choose **Archive risk**. Archived risks leave the registers and heat map; find them with the **Archived risks** filter in the register. Choose **Unarchive risk** to bring one back.
