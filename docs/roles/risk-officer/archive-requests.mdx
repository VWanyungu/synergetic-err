## Decide a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the notification">
    The bell in the top bar shows unread items. Open [Notifications](/common/notifications) to see each request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the risk">
    Select **View risk**. A banner at the top names who asked and when.
    <Frame><img src="/images/archive-request-banner.png" alt="Archive request banner" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or reject">
    **Accept** archives the risk. **Reject** keeps it in the register.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Archive or restore directly

On the right sidebar menu of a risk, click **Archive risk** to archive it or **Unarchive risk** to restore it. Archived risks leave the registers and heat map; find them with the **Archived** status filter in the register.
