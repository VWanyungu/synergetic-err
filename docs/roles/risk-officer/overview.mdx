title: "Risk Officer"
description: "Organisation-wide oversight, archive decisions and user management."
---

You see **every department and division** and can edit any risk. You also decide archive requests and manage users.

## Your routine

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Monitor the organisation" icon="chart-line" href="/roles/risk-officer/oversight">Dashboard, heat map and monthly reports across all departments.</Card>
  <Card title="Decide archive requests" icon="box-archive" href="/roles/risk-officer/archive-requests">Accept or reject requests from Risk Champions.</Card>
  <Card title="Manage users" icon="users" href="/roles/admin/managing-users">Create accounts and assign roles.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## What you can do

| Task | Notes |
|---|---|
| Create, edit, transfer and close any risk | Same tools as a [Risk Champion](/roles/department/overview), but in every department |
| Archive a risk directly | No request needed |
| Unarchive a risk | Gear menu on an archived risk |
| Manage users | Cannot change an Admin's account or grant Admin |

<Note>Only an Admin can manage Admin accounts or grant the Admin role. See [Admin](/roles/admin/overview).</Note>
