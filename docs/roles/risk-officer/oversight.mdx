---
title: "Oversight"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start on the dashboard">
    Check **Review compliance** and **Mitigation coverage**. Filter by **Department**, or set a **Period** to see a past month. See [Dashboard](/reference/dashboard).
    <Frame><img src="/images/dashboard.png" alt="Dashboard" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the pending reviews">
    Select the arrow on **Review compliance** to list risks not yet reviewed this month, then follow up with their owners.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the spread">
    Open the [heat map](/reference/heat-map) to see where High risks cluster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the monthly report">
    Open [Reports](/reference/reports), pick the month and use the download button for a PDF.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use the register's status filter (set to **Archived risks**) to find risks outside the active view.

<Note>All risks, except archived ones, get aggregated</Note>
