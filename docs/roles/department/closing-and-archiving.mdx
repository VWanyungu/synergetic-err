---
title: "Closing and archiving"
description: "Close a finished risk, or ask a Risk Officer to archive one."
---

## Close a risk

When mitigation is done, select **Close risk** beside the comment box. The risk becomes **Inactive** but still counts in past periods. To bring it back, select **Open risk** on a closed risk.

## Archive a risk

Archiving removes a risk from the active registers. You cannot archive directly; you ask a Risk Officer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the risk">
    Open the gear menu in the risk detail.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request archive">
    Choose **Request archive** and select **Send request**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/archive-request.png" alt="Archive request" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the decision">
    Risk Officers are notified and the risk stays in the register until they accept. You get a [notification](/common/notifications) when the request is accepted or rejected. A second request cannot be sent while one is pending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>A risk is Active until closed; closing and archiving are separate. A closed risk stays in the register until it is archived.</Note>
