---
title: "Creating a risk"
description: "Log a new risk with its assessment."
---

Select **New risk** in the top bar (or in a register).

<Frame><img src="/images/new-risk.png" alt="New risk form" /></Frame>

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Title** (required) | A short name for the risk. |
| **Description** | What could happen, what causes it, and what it would affect. |
| **Category** (required) | From the organisation-wide category list. |
| **Department** (required) | Decides the risk ID prefix, for example `COM-06`. |
| **Risk owner** | Defaults to you. |
| **Impact** and **Likelihood** | 1 to 5. The [rating](/reference/scoring) is calculated for you. |
| **Risk response** | Accept, Mitigate, Transfer or Avoid. |

<Tip>You can save without impact and likelihood, but the risk has no rating until both are set.</Tip>
