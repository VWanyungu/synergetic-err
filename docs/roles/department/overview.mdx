You work is scoped to your department. A Department Lead has the same permissions as a Risk Champion.

## Your routine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log new risks">
    Click **New risk** and fill in the form. See [Creating a risk](/roles/department/creating-a-risk).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan the response">
    Add mitigation plans. See [Mitigation plans and reviews](/roles/department/mitigation-and-review).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review every month">
    Click **Log review** on each open risk. See [Mitigation plans and reviews](/roles/department/mitigation-and-review).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close or archive">
    Close a risk to set its response to **Accept** and status to **Inactive**; ask a Risk Officer to archive one that should leave the register. See [Closing and archiving](/roles/department/closing-and-archiving).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Track review compliance and mitigation coverage | [Dashboard](/reference/dashboard) |
| See where your risks cluster | [Heat map](/reference/heat-map) |
| Download a monthly snapshot | [Reports](/reference/reports) |
| Move a risk to another department | **Risk detail**, gear menu, **Transfer risk** |

<Note>After a risk is transferred out, it becomes read only for your department.</Note>
