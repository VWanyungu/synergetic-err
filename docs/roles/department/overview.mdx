---
title: "Risk Champion and Department Lead"
description: "Maintain your department's risks, from logging to the monthly review."
---

You work inside **one department**. You see and edit only that department's risks, and you see the dashboard, heat map and reports for it alone. A Department Lead has the same permissions as a Risk Champion, so this guide covers both.

## Your routine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log new risks">
    Select **New risk** and fill in the form. See [Creating a risk](/roles/department/creating-a-risk).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan the response">
    Add mitigation plans with an owner, due date and status. See [Mitigation plans and reviews](/roles/department/mitigation-and-review).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review every month">
    Select **Log review** on each open risk. See [Mitigation plans and reviews](/roles/department/mitigation-and-review).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close or archive">
    Close a risk once its mitigation is done; ask a Risk Officer to archive one that should leave the register. See [Closing and archiving](/roles/department/closing-and-archiving).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Track review compliance and mitigation coverage | [Dashboard](/reference/dashboard) |
| See where your risks cluster | [Heat map](/reference/heat-map) |
| Download a monthly snapshot | [Reports](/reference/reports) |
| Move a risk to another department | **Risk detail**, gear menu, **Transfer risk** |

<Note>After a risk is transferred out, it becomes read only for your department.</Note>
