## Add a mitigation plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the risk">
    Find it in your [register](/common/registers) and open it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Mitigation plans">
    Open the **Mitigation plans** tab and select **Add mitigation plan**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/mitigation-add.png" alt="Adding a mitigation plan" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Give it a **title** (required), a description, an **owner**, a **due date** and a **status**. Save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Edit a plan later with the pencil icon on its card. A plan past its due date that is not complete is marked **overdue**.

<Frame><img src="/images/mitigation-plans.png" alt="Mitigation plans tab" /></Frame>

## Monthly review

The quickest way to review a risk is the **Log review** button.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the risk">
    Go through its rating, mitigation plans and recent activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Log review">
    In the **Review** section of the side panel, select **Log review**. **Last reviewed** updates to today and the review is recorded in **Activity**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/risk-sidebar.png" alt="Log review button in the side panel" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a comment if needed">
    Comments are optional notes for the record, and they do not log a review on their own. Posted comments cannot be edited or deleted, so add a new one to correct an earlier one.
    <Frame><img src="/images/risk-comment.png" alt="Adding a comment" /></Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use **Review compliance** on the [dashboard](/reference/dashboard) to see which of your risks are still waiting for a review this month.

## Keep the rating current

Use the gear icon beside **Rating** in the side panel to change impact or likelihood. Every change is recorded in **Activity** with the old and new values. See [Risk scoring](/reference/scoring).
