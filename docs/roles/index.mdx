What you can see and do depends on your role. You can check yours under **My access** on your [profile](/common/profile).

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Risk Champion / Department Lead" icon="clipboard-list" href="/roles/department/overview">Log and maintain your department's risks, mitigation plans and monthly reviews.</Card>
  <Card title="Division Lead" icon="eye" href="/roles/division-lead/overview">Read the risks affecting your division.</Card>
  <Card title="Risk Officer" icon="shield-check" href="/roles/risk-officer/overview">Oversee every department, decide archive requests and manage users.</Card>
  <Card title="Admin" icon="user-gear" href="/roles/admin/overview">Everything a Risk Officer does, plus managing Admins.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## At a glance

| | Risk Champion / Department Lead | Division Lead | Risk Officer | Admin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | Own department | Own division | Everything | Everything |
| Dashboard, heat map, reports | Own department | No | All | All |
| Create risks | Own department | No | Any | Any |
| Edit, transfer, close | Own department | No | Any | Any |
| Comment (counts as the review) | Yes | No | Yes | Yes |
| Archive | Sends a request | No | Directly | Directly |
| Decide archive requests | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Manage users | No | No | Yes | Yes, including Admins |

<Note>A Department Lead has exactly the same permissions as a Risk Champion.</Note>
