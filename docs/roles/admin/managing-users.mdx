title: "Managing users"
description: "Create accounts, assign roles and scope, and deactivate users."
---

<Note>The **Admin** menu is visible to Admins and Risk Officers. Only an Admin can change an Admin account, grant the Admin role or change a user's email.</Note>

<Frame><img src="/images/admin.png" alt="User management" /></Frame>

## Create a user

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select New user">
    Open **Admin** in the top bar and select **New user**.
    <Frame><img src="/images/admin-new-user.png" alt="New user form" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter their name, email and phone, then choose a role. Admin-created accounts start with a default password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set their scope">
    A Risk Champion or Department Lead **needs a department**. A Division Lead needs a **division**. Risk Officers and Admins need neither.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask them to reset the password">
    Tell them to use [Forgot password?](/common/resetting-password) the first time they sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit a user

Search by name or email, and filter by **Role** or **Status**. Open a user to edit their name, phone, role, department or division.

<Frame><img src="/images/admin-user-detail.png" alt="User detail" /></Frame>

<Note>Changing a role, department or division signs the person out so the change applies. They need to sign in again to continue.</Note>

## Roles

| Role | Scope | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Admin | Everything | Full control, including other Admins. |
| Risk Officer | Everything | Organisation-wide oversight and user management. |
| Risk Champion | One department | Maintains that department's risks. |
| Department Lead | One department | Same permissions as a Risk Champion. |
| Division Lead | One division | Read-only view of that division. |

## Deactivate a user

Open the user and deactivate them. They cannot sign in, but their history stays on every risk they touched. Deactivated users show struck through, with a **Deactivated** status.
