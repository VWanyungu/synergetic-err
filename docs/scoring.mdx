---
title: "Risk scoring"
description: "How ratings and levels are calculated."
---

**Rating = Impact × Likelihood**, each scored 1 to 5, so a rating is between 1 and 25. The system calculates it; it cannot be typed in.

| Rating | Level |
|---|---|
| 1 – 8 | Low |
| 9 – 14 | Medium |
| 15 – 25 | High |

The same rule is used on every screen (dashboard, heat map, registers, reports), so a risk never shows a different level in two places.

A risk with no impact or likelihood has no rating and no level until both are set.

## Risk response

Each risk records how you plan to deal with it: **Accept**, **Mitigate**, **Transfer** or **Avoid**.

## Status

A risk is **Active** until it is closed, after which it is **Inactive**. Closed risks still count in the registers and past reports for the periods they were open.
