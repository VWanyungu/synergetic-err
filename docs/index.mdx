---
title: "Enterprise Risk Register"
---

The Enterprise Risk Register (ERR) helps the organisation record and manage risks across departments and divisions. It brings together risk ratings, owners, mitigation plans, review records and activity history, with registers and dashboards to support ongoing oversight.

## Environments

| Production | Staging |
|---|---|
|The live register. [visit](https://synergetic-err-frontend.vercel.app/) | A volatile test copy where changes arrive before they go live. Its data may change or be cleared at any time. Seeing the current state of the app out of curiosity and testing a new feature before it reaches production. [visit](https://synergetic-err-frontend.vercel.app/)|

<Warning>Record real risks only in production. Anything you enter in staging is not part of the official register and does not appear in production.</Warning>

## Quickstart

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Use your work email and password. See [Signing in](/common/signing-in), or [reset your password](/common/resetting-password) if you cannot get in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do your first task">
    - **Risk Champion / Department Lead:** [log a risk](/roles/department/creating-a-risk).
    - **Division Lead:** open your [division register](/roles/division-lead/overview).
    - **Risk Officer / Admin:** open the [dashboard](/roles/risk-officer/oversight).
  </Step>
</Steps>
