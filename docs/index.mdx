title: "Enterprise Risk Register"
description: "One place to log, score, mitigate and review risks across Synergetic's departments and divisions."

The Enterprise Risk Register (ERR) replaces the spreadsheet. Every risk has an owner, a score, a mitigation plan and a full history, and the monthly review is driven from the same data.
