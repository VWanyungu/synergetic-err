title: "Enterprise Risk Register"
description: "One place to log, score, mitigate and review risks across Synergetic's departments and divisions."
---

The Enterprise Risk Register (ERR) replaces the spreadsheet. Every risk has an owner, a score, a mitigation plan and a full history, and the monthly review is driven from the same data.

## Two environments

The register runs in two places. They look the same, but they are used for different things.

| | Production | Staging |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | The live register. | A test copy where changes arrive before they go live. |
| **Use it for** | Your real, day-to-day work: logging risks, mitigation plans and reviews. | Seeing the current state of the app out of curiosity, and testing a new feature before it reaches production. |
| **Data** | Real, and it feeds the dashboard and monthly reports. | Test data. It may change or be cleared at any time. |

<Warning>Record real risks only in production. Anything you enter in staging is not part of the official register and does not appear in production.</Warning>
